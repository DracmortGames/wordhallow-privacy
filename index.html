WordHallow Privacy Policy

A game by Dracmort · Last updated: 6 October 2026

The short version

  • WordHallow has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress, coins, items and settings are saved only on your device. Dracmort does not run servers and does not receive this data.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. The purchase library (Unity IAP) reports purchase events to Unity Technologies.
  • We do not sell your personal information. WordHallow is made for players aged 13 and over.

1. Who we are

WordHallow (Android package com.dracmort.wordhallow) is a word-association card game developed and published by Dracmort ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at dracmortgames@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The game keeps a save file in its private app storage so you can continue where you left off. It contains:

  • your level progress, the level in progress and your moves on it;
  • your coin balance and your Hint and Undo tokens;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, effects);
  • how many rewarded ads you have watched today, so the daily limits work;
  • the store's transaction IDs of purchases already delivered, so a purchase is never granted twice.

This file never leaves your device and is not sent to Dracmort. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes it. Because there is no account or cloud save, we cannot restore lost progress.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

WordHallow shows interstitial ads between levels and optional rewarded ads (for example "+5 moves", a free Hint or a free Undo). Ads are provided by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), operated by Google. When ads are requested and shown, Google's SDK collects and processes, according to Google's own disclosures:

  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • App interactions, such as ads viewed and tapped;
  • Diagnostics, such as information about crashes and performance of the SDK.

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and it is sent to Google encrypted in transit. Ads may be personalised based on your activity unless you opt out.

Android ad services (Privacy Sandbox)

On Android devices that support it, the Google Mobile Ads SDK can also use Android's own ad services ("Privacy Sandbox"), which is why the app lists the ACCESS_ADSERVICES_* permissions in section 6. These system features are designed to make ads work with less cross-app tracking:

  • Ad ID from ad services – the advertising identifier provided through Android's ad services;
  • Attribution reporting – lets Android measure whether an ad was seen or tapped and led to an action, without sharing a cross-app identifier;
  • Topics – Android works out a few broad interest topics on your device from the apps you use, and an ad request may receive some of them for interest-based ads.

You can review or turn these off in your phone's ad privacy settings (on recent Android versions: Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads; the path varies by device).

Consent and your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent message (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are used, and you can make your choice there.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play and Unity IAP)

You can buy coins, Hint tokens and Undo tokens. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy; we never receive your payment card, bank or billing details. The game receives only the product you bought and the store's transaction ID and receipt, which it uses to deliver your items once and stores on your device (see section 2).

The Google Play Billing Library talks to the Play Store app on your phone to show and complete purchases, and includes Google's data-transport component, which can send technical information about purchase flows to Google.

Purchases go through the Unity In-App Purchasing library by Unity Technologies. When you start, complete or cancel a purchase, this library sends purchase event data to Unity, including:

  • the product, its price and currency, and the store transaction ID;
  • device information: device model, operating-system version, language and locale settings, total storage size and device start-up time;
  • app information: package name, app version and install time;
  • identifiers made available to the library, such as a Unity installation ID and, where available, the device's advertising ID.

Unity processes this data under the Unity Privacy Policy.

5. No analytics or diagnostics of our own

WordHallow is built with the Unity engine. In the released game, Unity Analytics, Unity's engine diagnostics and its crash and performance reporting are all turned off, and we do not add any other analytics or crash-reporting service. The only data that leaves your device is what the Google AdMob, Google Play and Unity In-App Purchasing services described above collect.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise (GPS) location.
  • We do not run our own analytics or servers, and we do not collect or store your data ourselves.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

8. Children

WordHallow is designed and marketed for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us at dracmortgames@gmail.com and we will help remove it.

9. Retention, deletion and your rights

  • On your device: data stays until you uninstall the game or clear its storage.
  • Google and Unity: they keep data according to their own policies. You can use the controls in section 3 and contact them through the links above.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, privacy laws in US states such as California, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data ourselves, most requests are best directed to Google or Unity, but you can always contact us and we will help.

10. Security

Your save file is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Data sent by the Google and Unity SDKs is transmitted to those companies using their own security measures.

11. Changes to this policy

If the game starts using new services or handles data differently, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's store listing.
